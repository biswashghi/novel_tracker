# Novel Tracker support

Novel Tracker saves the web novel chapter you stopped on and keeps your reading
progress up to date as you move through a series. This page is the place to ask
questions, report problems, and request help.

## Contact

- **Report a bug or ask a question:** open an issue at
  [github.com/biswashghi/novel_tracker/issues](https://github.com/biswashghi/novel_tracker/issues).
  This is the fastest route and does not require any personal information beyond
  a GitHub account.
- **Email:** novel-tracker@bghimire.com

Expect a reply within a few business days. When reporting a problem, it helps to
include your platform (Safari on iPhone/iPad/Mac, Chrome, or Firefox), the app or
extension version, the reading site involved, and what you expected to happen.

## Getting started

### iPhone and iPad

1. Install Novel Tracker from the App Store.
2. Open **Settings → Apps → Safari → Extensions**, select **Novel Tracker**, and
   turn it on.
3. Allow Novel Tracker to access the reading sites you use.
4. In Safari, open a chapter page and tap the extensions button in the address
   bar, then choose **Novel Tracker**.

### Mac

Install from the App Store, then enable Novel Tracker in **Safari → Settings →
Extensions**.

### Chrome and Firefox

Install from the Chrome Web Store or Firefox Add-ons. No extra setup is needed.

## Frequently asked questions

### Where is my library?

Open the Novel Tracker button in the browser toolbar and choose **Library**. The
library page is where you browse, search, sort, edit, delete, and reopen saved
novels, and where JSON backup export and import live.

On iPhone and iPad the library opens as a normal Safari tab, because iOS Safari
does not provide a separate extension settings screen.

### Do I need an account?

No. Novel Tracker works entirely without one, and your reading data stays in your
browser profile. Signing in is only needed to sync your library across devices.

### How do I save a chapter?

Open a chapter page on a supported site, open the Novel Tracker popup, and choose
to save. On supported sites your progress then updates automatically as you read
further into the same novel.

### Which sites are supported?

Royal Road, Patreon, Wuxiaworld, NovelBin, ScribbleHub, Creative Novels, Light
Novels Translations, Shin Translations, and Chikari. Other sites fall back to
generic page information and can still be saved manually.

### I signed in with Apple but my library looks empty

Signing in with Apple and signing in with Google create two separate Novel
Tracker accounts, even when both use the same email address. Each account keeps
its own synced library. Sign in again with the same provider you used originally
to see that library. Your local, on-device library is never affected by which
provider you choose.

### How do I back up or move my library?

Use **Export** on the library page to download a JSON backup, and **Import** to
restore one. Backups are plain JSON files you can keep anywhere.

### How do I delete my account?

You can delete your Novel Tracker account and everything synced to it at any
time:

- **iPhone and iPad:** open the Novel Tracker app, sign in if you are not already,
  and choose **Delete Account**.
- **Any browser:** open the library page and choose **Delete account** in the
  account area.

Deleting removes your synced library and your Novel Tracker account itself. If
you signed in with Apple, Novel Tracker also revokes its Sign in with Apple
token, so the app stops appearing under **Settings → Your Name → Sign in with
Apple**. The library stored locally in your browser is not touched, and you can
export it first if you want to keep it.

### How do I sign out without deleting anything?

Choose **Sign out** in the app or on the library page. Your local library stays
on the device.
