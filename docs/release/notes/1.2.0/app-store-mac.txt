Novel Tracker does more, and five more sites are supported.

FIVE MORE SITES
Archive of Our Own, Wattpad, Webnovel, NovelFire and ReadNovelFull are now followed chapter by chapter, joining Royal Road, Scribble Hub, Wuxiaworld and more.

SAVE FASTER
• Save the chapter you're on with Option-Shift-S, or right-click the page and choose "Save chapter to Novel Tracker".
• After your first save, Novel Tracker tells you that from then on it follows you from chapter to chapter by itself.

A BETTER LIBRARY
• Refreshed design, with a dark theme.
• A reading-activity heatmap of the past year.
• Recently deleted, with undo.
• New sorts: Least recent, Newly added, Most chapters read.
• "Continue reading" in the popup jumps back into your latest novels.
• Export your library as a spreadsheet (CSV), or back it up and restore it from the Backup menu.
• The library updates by itself while it's open.

FIXES
• Different novels on the same site are no longer mixed together, and your bookmark no longer jumps to a novel's contents page.
• Webnovel's phone reader is tracked chapter by chapter.
• Signing out now ends your session on our servers as well.
